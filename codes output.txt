----------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTE ON CRAMER'S V
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The Cramer's V values reported here use the 1xk goodness-of-fit variant,
not the standard rxk contingency-table version. For a goodness-of-fit
test, the formula is V = sqrt(chi^2 / (n * (k-1))), where k is the number
of categories. This is appropriate for assessing how well observed
frequencies match a theoretical distribution (e.g., geometric).

----------------------------------------------------------------------
STATISTICAL POWER CAVEAT
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The chi-square tests within each group have only 2-3 degree(s) of
freedom (compared to 7 in the pooled test), with roughly one-third the
sample size.

The tercile result itself has essentially no power to confirm or
rule out anything. 'Not rejected' here is close to uninformative.
